RAW [2023]

Raw - Let It Bleed is a health store designed exclusively for
women, offering natural remedies to alleviate the weight and
strain of period pain - without the clichés that claim, "periods
are the best time of the month." The brand's illustrations draw
inspiration from the female body and organs, encouraging
women to share their real experiences.
