UMAMI [2022]

This book explores umami, the elusive sixth taste recognized in
Japanese cuisine. Divided into three chapters - Ramen, Sushi,
and Bento Boxes - it delves into the essence of these dishes
through a minimalist, Japanese - inspired aesthetic. The book
features hand - drawn illustrations that reflect the precision and
artistry of Japanese food design.
I was responsible for every aspect of the project, from writing
and translation to editing and illustration, ensuring a cohesive
and immersive experience.
